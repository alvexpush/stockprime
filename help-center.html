01

Getting started

Set up and understand your account.

How to create an account +

Select Create account, enter your details, choose a secure password, and follow the verification prompts. You can sign in as soon as registration is complete.

KYC verification process +

Open your account verification area, provide the requested identity details and documents, then submit them for review. You will see the current review status in your dashboard.

Platform overview +

Your dashboard brings together your wallet, recent transactions, investments, market activity, profile, and support access in one place.

02

Account & security

Manage your profile and protect access.

Updating your profile +

Sign in, open your profile settings, update the available fields, and save your changes. Contact support if a verified identity field needs correction.

Security settings +

Use a unique password and enable every account security option available to you. Never share your password, verification code, or recovery details.

Notification preferences +

Manage available account and transaction alerts from your profile settings. Important security and payment notices may remain enabled for your protection.

03

Wallet & payments

Fund, track, and withdraw securely.

Funding your wallet +

Open Deposit in your dashboard, select a supported method, enter the amount and transaction reference, then submit it for verification.

Supported cryptocurrencies +

The deposit screen shows the payment methods currently supported by the platform, including available cryptocurrency and bank transfer options.

Withdrawal process +

Choose Withdraw, enter the amount, method, and destination, then review the fee before submitting. Pending withdrawals remain visible in your transaction history.

Transaction fees +

Any applicable fee is shown before you confirm a transaction. Review the amount, fee, and destination carefully before submission.

04

Investments & trading

Understand plans and monitor positions.

Investment plans +

Compare each plan's minimum, duration, risk level, and projected return before investing. Projections are estimates and should not be treated as guaranteed outcomes.

Portfolio management +

Your dashboard summarizes invested value, current value, projected profit, and active positions so you can monitor performance from one view.

Market analysis +

Use current quotes, price movement, and market news as research inputs. Consider your goals and risk tolerance before placing an order.
